Help and support

How can we help?

For support, subscription questions, or feedback, email cheng.yang.chem@gmail.com.

Before contacting support

  • Update to the latest TestFlight or App Store build.
  • For purchase access, open PhaseGlow Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • For reminders, confirm notifications are enabled for PhaseGlow in iPhone Settings.

Managing local data

Edit or delete an individual check-in from Insights. Remove an attached photo from that check-in. To erase the local profile, routine, history, and photos, use Delete all PhaseGlow data in Settings.

Health concerns

PhaseGlow does not diagnose or treat skin conditions. For persistent, painful, sudden, or concerning symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional.

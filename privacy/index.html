Privacy Policy

PhaseGlow is designed to provide a useful skincare experience without sending your cycle or skin history to PhaseGlow.

Information stored on your device

The app can store the cycle information you enter, skin check-ins, breakout locations, notes, routines, product names and ingredient families, reminder preferences, forecast snapshots, and optional skin photos. This information is stored in PhaseGlow’s local app storage on your device.

Photos

Photos are optional and available only when you select them through Apple’s system photo picker. PhaseGlow copies the selected image into its private local app storage. PhaseGlow does not request broad access to your photo library and does not upload photos to a PhaseGlow server.

Data collection

PhaseGlow does not operate an account system or backend service and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. PhaseGlow does not collect or sell personal data. The app does not use HealthKit.

Apple services

Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Notifications are scheduled locally through iOS. Apple may process related information under Apple’s own privacy policy. Depending on your iOS backup settings, local app data may be included in an encrypted computer backup or iCloud device backup.

Your controls

You can edit or delete individual check-ins and attached photos. You can delete all PhaseGlow data from Settings in the app. Deleting the app also removes its local app storage from that device, subject to any device backups managed by you.

Children

PhaseGlow is not directed to children under 13. It is a general skincare and wellness companion and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If PhaseGlow’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.
